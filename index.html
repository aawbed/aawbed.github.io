Featured Project 2025 – 2026

Kenya Airways (KQ) Sales & Order Intelligence System

A web-based platform for managing water sales orders and warehouse inventory stock levels, designed around Kenya Airways operational workflows.

  • User Permissions: 4 distinct roles (Sales Agents, Warehouse Officers, Managers, Administrators) to keep data secure and organized.
  • Account Security: Automatic lockout after 5 incorrect password attempts and 30-minute idle session logouts.
  • Automated Reports: Generates 16% VAT invoices and one-click PDF and CSV reports to cut down on manual paperwork.
  • Order Forecasting: Implemented time-series forecasting (SARIMAX) to estimate daily water demand and flag abnormal bulk orders.
